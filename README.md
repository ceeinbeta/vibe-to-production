## Hola, friend!

Building an app with AI has never been easier. You can turn an idea into a working prototype faster than ever, but getting that prototype ready for real users takes more than making sure the app runs.

Before you launch, you need to think about security, testing, reliability, performance, infrastructure, privacy, and what happens when something inevitably goes wrong.

That's why I created **Vibe to Production**: a practical checklist and AI-assisted audit guide to help you identify gaps before shipping your next app.

Whether you're building your first side project, launching an MVP, or turning an AI-generated prototype into a real product, this resource helps you look beyond the interface and check what's happening under the hood.

It's designed to help you:

- Identify production-readiness gaps before launch.
- Catch security risks and potential data leaks.
- Verify that testing and deployment processes are in place.
- Reduce the risk of unexpected costs and data loss.
- Evaluate AI-specific risks and failure scenarios.
- Build more reliable products that users can trust.

This checklist is a starting point, not a guarantee of security or production readiness. Your app may need additional controls depending on its architecture, data sensitivity, scale, and business requirements.

Let's build smarter and ship more confidently.

## What's in this repo

```
vibe-to-production/
├── README.md       # Introduction, overview, and how to use the project
├── CHECKLIST.md    # The complete production-readiness checklist
├── SKILL.md        # AI audit instructions and required report format
└── LICENSE         # MIT License
```

- **[CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md)** has seven sections, starting with the high-risk checks (costs, backups, secrets, access control), then testing, monitoring, infrastructure, AI-specific checks, privacy and accessibility, and launch polish.
- **[SKILL.md](SKILL.md)** tells an AI coding assistant how to audit your real codebase against the checklist and report each item as Done, Missing, or Can't verify, with evidence.

## How to use it

**On your own:** open [CHECKLIST.md](CHECKLIST.md), start with section 1, and tick off each item. Skip items that genuinely don't apply.

**With an AI coding assistant:**

1. Add the checklist and audit instructions to your repository.
2. Open your project in your preferred AI coding assistant.
3. Ask it to run the Vibe Code Launch Check against the actual codebase.
4. Review the evidence and the Can't verify items.
5. Approve a remediation plan before allowing changes.
6. Run the audit again after implementing fixes.

The quality of the report depends on the files, tools, permissions, and operational evidence available to the auditor. No automated checklist can guarantee that an application is secure or production-ready.
